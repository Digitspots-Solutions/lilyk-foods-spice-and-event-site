About Us

Lilyk Foods Spice and Event is a proudly Nigerian food manufacturing company based in Kubuwa, Abuja. We specialize in crafting premium spice blends that capture the rich, authentic flavours of Nigerian cuisine, alongside providing professional event catering services that turn your celebrations into unforgettable experiences. Our commitment to quality ingredients, traditional recipes, and exceptional customer service has made us a preferred choice for families and event planners across the Federal Capital Territory. Whether you're stocking your kitchen with our signature spice blends or planning your next big celebration, Lilyk Foods brings passion, expertise, and authentic Nigerian taste to every product and event we serve.

Our Services

Premium Spice Manufacturing

Our signature spice blends are carefully crafted using the finest local ingredients. From pepper soup spice to suya seasoning, our products bring authentic Nigerian flavour to every meal. Available in various sizes for home cooks and commercial kitchens.

Event Catering Services

Make your celebration memorable with our professional catering. We handle weddings, birthdays, corporate events, and traditional ceremonies with delicious Nigerian cuisine and impeccable service. Custom menus available to suit your taste and budget.

Bulk & Wholesale Supply

We supply restaurants, hotels, and retailers with consistent quality spice products. Competitive pricing and reliable delivery across Abuja and surrounding areas.
